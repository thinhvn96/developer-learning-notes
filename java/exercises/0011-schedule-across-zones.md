# Exercise 0011: schedule across zones

Companion to [lesson 11](../lessons/0011-java-time.html).

A meeting is at 09:00 Europe/Berlin on the last Sunday of March, when clocks change. Use `ZonedDateTime` to compute the same meeting a week earlier and later, and print each in UTC and in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh. What is the `Duration` between the week-earlier and week-later meetings, and why is it not exactly 14 days worth of hours?
