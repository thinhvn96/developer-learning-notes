# Exercise 0003: choose a broker

Companion to [lesson 3](../lessons/0003-queue-versus-log.html). · [Tiếng Việt](0003-choose-a-broker.vi.md)

For each system, choose RabbitMQ or Kafka (or neither) and defend it with documented behavior: (a) image-resize jobs with retries and a dead-letter store; (b) a clickstream feeding analytics, fraud detection and search indexing independently; (c) order events that a new service must be able to replay from last week; (d) request/response style RPC. What would change your mind?
