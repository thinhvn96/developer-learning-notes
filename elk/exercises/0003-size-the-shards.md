# Exercise 0003: size the shards

Companion to [lesson 3](../lessons/0003-shards-and-replicas.html).

Given a daily log index that grows to 30 GB, and a 3-node cluster, propose primary and replica counts and defend them. What breaks if you need more primaries later? What is the effect of setting replicas to 0 during a bulk load, and what is the risk? (Look for guidance in the docs and say what you could not find.)
