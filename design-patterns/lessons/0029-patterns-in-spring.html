Retrieval practice

Answer from memory. One attempt each.

1. Spring's singleton scope is per...?

It is per-container and per-bean, not per ClassLoader.[1]

2. Why did self-invocation skip the transaction?

Only calls through the proxy are intercepted.[3]

3. How do you get a fresh prototype in a singleton?

The demo's ObjectProvider produced ids 4 and 5.[1]

4. Who instantiates and injects beans?

The container instantiates, assembles and manages beans.[2]
