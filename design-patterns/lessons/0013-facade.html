Facade

Design Patterns · Lesson 0013 · step 13 of 30 · Core · ~10 minutes · Home · Design Patterns ladder · Roadmap

Today's win: offer one simple entry point in front of a complex subsystem and avoid turning it into a god object.

Intent

Facade is "a structural design pattern that provides a simplified interface to a library, a framework, or any other complex set of classes." Use it for a limited but straightforward interface to a complex subsystem, or to structure a subsystem into layers.[1]

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Isolates your code from a subsystem's complexity

Cons

  • A facade can become a god object coupled to all classes of an app

(From the pattern's page.)[1] Adapter usually wraps one object while Facade works with a subsystem; Abstract Factory is an alternative when you only want to hide creation; Mediator centralizes communication actively while Facade only simplifies access; a facade is often a Singleton.
