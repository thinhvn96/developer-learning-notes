SOLID in one line each

  • SRP: one reason to change per class.
  • OCP: open for extension, closed for modification.
  • LSP: subtypes substitutable for their base types.
  • ISP: do not depend on methods you do not use.
  • DIP: depend on abstractions, not concretes.

Look-alikes

  • Strategy (client picks, no transitions) vs State (states trigger transitions).
  • Decorator/Proxy (same interface) vs Adapter (different) vs Facade (simplified subsystem interface).
  • Template Method (inheritance) vs Strategy (composition).
  • Mediator (central coordinator) vs Observer (one-way subscriptions).
