CLI

kafka-topics.sh --bootstrap-server B --create --topic T --partitions 6 --replication-factor 3
kafka-topics.sh --bootstrap-server B --describe --topic T
kafka-topics.sh --bootstrap-server B --alter --topic T --partitions 12      # remaps keys!
kafka-configs.sh --bootstrap-server B --alter --entity-type topics --entity-name T --add-config retention.ms=604800000
kafka-consumer-groups.sh --bootstrap-server B --describe --group G           # offsets, lag
kafka-consumer-groups.sh --bootstrap-server B --group G --reset-offsets --to-earliest --topic T --execute
kafka-share-groups.sh --bootstrap-server B --describe --group G
kafka-metadata-quorum.sh --bootstrap-server B describe --status
kafka-acls.sh --bootstrap-server B --add --allow-principal User:svc --consumer --topic T --group G
kafka-producer-perf-test.sh --topic T --num-records 500000 --throughput -1 --record-size 100 \
  --command-property bootstrap.servers=B acks=all linger.ms=20 compression.type=zstd

Choosing

QuestionPick
Ordered per key, replayable?consumer group
Independent jobs, scale past partition count, per-record retry?share group (4.2+)
Rich routing, per-message TTL, priorities?RabbitMQ (queue vs log)
Transform topic to topic with state?Kafka Streams
Move data to/from a database or search engine?Kafka Connect
Survive a region loss?MirrorMaker 2 (async, small RPO)
