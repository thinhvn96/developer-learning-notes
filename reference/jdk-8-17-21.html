JDK 8, 17 and 21 cheat sheet

Home · Reference · print-friendly · Java ladder · Java interview Q&A

A feature is listed under the JDK that finalized it. JDK 17 includes everything finalized in 9 through 17; JDK 21 includes everything through 21. LTS releases per Oracle: 8, 11, 17, 21, 25 (support roadmap).

Verified boundaries

Compiled while writing this course: the JDK 8 examples with javac --release 8, the JDK 17 example with --release 17, the JDK 21 example with --release 21. Compiling the JDK 21 example with --release 17 fails ("patterns in switch statements are not supported in -source 17").

Version-independent must-knows

  • equals/hashCode contract; HashMap defaults (capacity 16, load factor 0.75), not synchronized. Lesson 1
  • Happens-before: monitor unlock/lock, volatile write/read, Thread.start, join. Lesson 5
  • GC defaults: G1 on server-class machines, else Serial; max heap 1/4 of RAM. Lesson 7
